## 3. The 8-Stage Multi-Stage Validation Standard

No code, configuration, or document is accepted into master without passing all 8 stages:
*   **Stage 1 (Generate):** Produce clean, strictly typed, memory-safe code or architectural specification.
*   **Stage 2 (Self-Review):** Line-by-line static analysis and syntax verification.
*   **Stage 3 (Gap Identification):** Attack simulation, edge-case probing, and boundary analysis.
*   **Stage 4 (Improve):** Implement immediate hardening, defensive bounds, and exception handling.
*   **Stage 5 (Objective Validation):** Verify mathematical adherence to PRD and Architecture invariants.
*   **Stage 6 (Risk Assessment):** Assess performance overhead, memory safety, and verifier limits.
*   **Stage 7 (Final Optimization):** Profile execution speed, verify zero allocations in hot paths.
*   **Stage 8 (Deliver):** Execute automated test suite, commit to Git, and push to GitHub.

## 4. Autonomous Escalation Thresholds (Strict Escalation Boundary)

Per Operating Principle 7, the council will proceed autonomously **EXCEPT** when:
1.  A decision causes irreversible production data destruction.
2.  Unbudgeted financial expenditure is incurred.
3.  Legal or regulatory liability is triggered.
4.  Multiple equally viable architectural paths yield diametrically opposed business models.

All routine engineering, implementation, refactoring, agent allocation, scheduling, and technical optimizations proceed autonomously 24/7.
